# Alice Chen

Alice co-founded Atlas Robotics in late 2024 and runs the company day-to-day as
CEO. She came out of a decade in industrial automation — three years at a
warehouse-systems company on the controls team, then four years leading a
robot-arm motion-planning group, then two years as VP of Engineering at a
mid-stage logistics startup before splitting off to start Atlas with Bob
Martinez and Carlos Yim. Her instincts run toward shipping early, watching what
breaks, and revising the plan from data — which is why the 2026-Q2 pivot from
consumer to warehouse focus happened as fast as it did. See
[[decisions/2026-03-12-pivot-to-warehouse]] for the decision record.

## What she owns

- Company strategy, fundraising, and board communications.
- Direct ownership of the flagship [[projects/atlas-1]] roadmap — she still
  reviews every quarterly milestone personally.
- Hiring above the senior-engineer level. Carlos owns the engineering hiring
  pipeline below that line.
- Public communication: press, investor updates, customer-facing keynotes at
  industry events.
- Final sign-off on pivots, kills, and reorgs.

## Working style

Async-first; she dislikes status standups and prefers written updates that she
can read on her own schedule. The Monday "weekly written" thread on Linear is
her doing — every project lead writes a 200-word update by Sunday EOD, she
reads them Monday morning, and the team converges over async comments rather
than a meeting.
