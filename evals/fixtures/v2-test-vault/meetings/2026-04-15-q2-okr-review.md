# 2026-Q2 OKR Review

Quarterly objectives-and-key-results review with the three founders. The
context coming in: we had just executed the consumer-to-warehouse pivot in
March (see [[decisions/2026-03-12-pivot-to-warehouse]]) and needed to retune
Q2 metrics so they reflected the new product positioning rather than the old
consumer roadmap.

## Agenda

1. Review Q1 OKR results — three of seven met, two partial, two missed.
2. Decide which Q2 OKRs survive the pivot and which need to be rewritten.
3. Reset the pilot-count target for [[projects/atlas-1]] given the warehouse
   focus.
4. Discuss whether Beacon (R&D side bet) gets paused for the quarter.

## Decisions

- **Pilot count for Atlas-1:** reduced from 12 to 8 for the quarter. The four
  consumer pilots that were already scheduled get converted to warehouse
  trials at the same sites where possible; the remaining slots go to new
  warehouse-only customers.
- **New reliability metric:** "two-shift uptime" — 16 hours of continuous
  warehouse operation with zero human interventions. Replaces the previous
  "demo readiness" subjective metric. Owner: Carlos Yim.
- **Beacon paused for Q2.** Bob's two engineers on Beacon roll back to the
  Atlas-1 perception team for the quarter. Reviewed again at the Q3 OKR cycle.
- **Spire (warehouse-ops product line)** gets its own dedicated PM hire in Q2
  rather than being a side-project of Alice's. Sourcing this week. See
  [[people/alice-chen]] for the hiring pipeline notes.

The room agreed quickly on the pilot reduction — the data from the February
pilots had already made the case. The reliability metric took longer; we
