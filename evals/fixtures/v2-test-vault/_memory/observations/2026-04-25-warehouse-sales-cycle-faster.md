# Warehouse sales cycles close ~4× faster than consumer

Per the pivot decision record, consumer prospects took 90+ days from first
contact to pilot agreement; warehouse operators reached out to
opportunistically were closing in under 30 days. This 4× delta in cycle
length was one of three primary signals that drove the pivot.

Operational implication: pipeline forecasting should now use a 30-day
median close window, not the 90-day window that was baked into the old
consumer plan.
