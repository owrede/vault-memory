# Working with Alice — communication preferences brief

**Async-first.** Default to written threads on Linear. The Monday weekly
written update (200 words, due Sunday EOD) is the canonical channel for
status. Live recurring meetings should be avoided unless the topic is
genuinely synchronous (decision, debate, customer call).

**Final sign-off.** Pivots, kills, and reorgs require Alice's explicit
sign-off. Senior-and-above hires also require her sign-off; Carlos owns
hiring below that line.

**Personal-review threads.** Alice still personally reviews the Atlas-1
roadmap and every quarterly milestone — do not surprise her in those
reviews. Surface risks in the Sunday written update, not at the milestone
gate.
