# Customer Pilot Playbook

How Atlas Robotics runs a warehouse pilot, from first-call through
graduation. Owned by [[people/priya-rao]]; reviewed quarterly. This
playbook is the source of truth for the eight Q2 pilot sites — six in
the warehouse vertical (four new, two converted from pre-pivot pilots),
and two legacy commercial sites being wound down.

## Stages

### Stage 1 — Qualification (1–2 weeks)

- 30-min discovery call with operations lead at the prospective
  warehouse.
- Site fitness questionnaire (zone sizes, pallet types, shift schedule).
- Decision to proceed routes to [[people/alice-chen]] for sign-off.

### Stage 2 — Onboarding (2 weeks)

- Site survey by Priya (in person, mandatory).
- Logistics handover to [[people/quinn-vega]] for shipping and spare
  kit.
- Field-tech contract executed (vendor varies by region).

### Stage 3 — Pilot operation (8–12 weeks)

- Day 1 install: Carlos ([[people/carlos-yim]]) on-site for
  commissioning.
- Weekly cadence call: Priya leads, customer ops lead attends.
- Field observations logged daily; weekly summary to engineering.
- Reliability metric reporting weekly to
  [[projects/atlas-1-reliability-program]].

### Stage 4 — Graduation or wind-down

- Graduation criteria: customer signs renewal LOI + 4 consecutive weeks
  of stable two-shift uptime. Handoff to [[people/dana-park]] for Spire
  product engagement.
- Wind-down criteria: customer requests exit, reliability below 50% of
  target for 4 weeks, or strategic misalignment (e.g. the two
  consumer pilots that wound down post-pivot per
  [[decisions/2026-03-12-pivot-to-warehouse]]).
